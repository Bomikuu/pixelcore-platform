# Live sales workspace

Mode: Operate. Extend the existing workspace, orders and product-request records. No new visual identity or fabricated revenue.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A daily sales desk with actionable queues, not an inventory of modules.

OWN-WORLD: Existing Manrope, warm surfaces, deep-green sidebar, Shadcn controls, restrained semantic attention colors.

STORY: Read paid-order performance, identify unfinished work, contact the customer and resolve the record.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Live controls above a compact metric strip; sales graph beside an attention list; paginated activity below. Sidebar leads with Live dashboard, Orders, Product requests. Requests show overdue context before routine details.

FORM: User-specified extension of the established admin; no direction seed needed. Thirty-second refresh preserves filters, pagination and open work. On mobile, rows stack and actions remain visible.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Verification boundary: user retains backend/browser testing; required Shadcn compilation and one detector pass only. No delegated agents.
